# Antidote Purpose

## Purpose statement

Antidote exists to investigate whether a person can describe the state they are
in, the transition they need in a particular moment, and the sonic meanings
that feel personally relevant, then use those descriptions to create an
inspectable generative-audio journey and learn carefully from the response.

## Need

Most music recommendation and generation systems optimize population-level
similarity, engagement, genre, or expressed emotion. They do not preserve a
person's idiosyncratic sonic language, model a transition across time, or keep
the intended journey distinguishable from the audio that was generated and the
experience that was actually felt.

## Beneficiaries

- people exploring music for reflection, regulation, expression, or creative
  self-inquiry;
- researchers studying personalized affective and generative-audio systems;
- engineers building interpretable, local-first adaptive media tools;
- future Ego Hygiene surfaces that need an explicitly consented audio-session
  capability.

## Enduring value

Antidote's enduring value is not one model or one therapeutic claim. It is a
portable, evidence-disciplined method for translating moment-specific semantic
intent into an acoustic trajectory, preserving what happened, and learning a
personal mapping without concealing uncertainty or removing human agency.

## Scope boundaries

Antidote owns its research source, semantic and acoustic domain contracts,
prototype, experiment definitions, response model, provenance, and publication
evidence. It does not diagnose, prescribe, claim clinical efficacy, replace
therapy or medication, silently inspect private history, or treat a generated
audio artifact as a medical intervention.

The originating cathartic experience is hypothesis-generating evidence for why
the question matters. It is not evidence of mechanism, efficacy, or
generalizability.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- **Observed:** The repository contains a reproducible research-paper workspace,
  source and claim ledgers, a conceptual semantic–acoustic–response loop, and a
  gated publication hub.
- **Decided for the prototype:** The first executable system is a transparent,
  local-first research instrument, not a clinical product.
- **Proposed:** A future Ego Hygiene module may provide explicitly selected
  journal or therapy-session context through a versioned public contract.
- **Open question:** How much benefit comes from personal semantic mappings,
  temporal journey structure, generation quality, expectancy, context, or their
  interaction?
