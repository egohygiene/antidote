# Antidote Manifesto

## Declaration

We believe music can become more than a generic recommendation or a prompt
returned as an opaque file. It can become a transparent collaboration among a
person, their present moment, a carefully described journey, a generative
system, and the evidence of what was actually experienced.

## We stand for

- the person who exists now, not only the profile accumulated before;
- sonic language that can hold metaphor, contradiction, culture, memory, and
  meaning without pretending they are universal acoustic laws;
- generation that invites imagination and interpretation that respects the
  limits of evidence;
- local ownership, explicit consent, interruption, correction, and recovery;
- tools that expose their plans, models, uncertainty, and lineage;
- research that can honor a profound personal experience without turning it
  into an unsupported promise to others.

## We reject

- mining private writing because more context might improve an output;
- converting diagnoses, brain regions, or frequencies into deterministic sonic
  prescriptions;
- equating music's expressed emotion with the listener's felt response;
- engagement optimization disguised as care;
- autonomous experimentation without protocol, consent, stop rules, and human
  review;
- clinical language used to make an early prototype sound more certain than it
  is.

## Commitment

We will make Antidote emotionally ambitious and epistemically humble. We will
preserve each person's authority over meaning, keep painful and adverse
responses visible, and revise the system when stronger evidence or lived
experience contradicts its assumptions.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- **Observed:** Music can be deeply meaningful while its effects remain
  heterogeneous and context-dependent.
- **Decided for the prototype:** Emotional intensity is not automatically
  success; the person evaluates whether an experience was wanted or helpful.
- **Proposed:** Antidote may eventually become an Ego Hygiene module after the
  standalone research boundary is proven.
- **Open question:** How should the interface distinguish catharsis, overwhelm,
  resonance, and later benefit without reducing them to one score?
