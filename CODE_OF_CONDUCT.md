# Code of Conduct

Antidote is a research-oriented open-source project concerning personal
experience, emotion, mental health, music, and AI. Contributors are expected to
keep discussion respectful, technically grounded, scientifically honest, and
careful with lived experience.

## Expected behavior

- engage with curiosity, clarity, empathy, and professionalism;
- focus on evidence, reproducibility, reviewable changes, and explicit
  uncertainty;
- distinguish disagreement with a claim from judgment of a person's experience;
- assume good faith while making scientific and technical conflicts visible;
- help keep the repository accessible to contributors with different
  backgrounds, abilities, identities, and experience levels;
- protect private or sensitive information and obtain permission before sharing.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, or discriminatory language;
- using diagnoses, trauma, spiritual beliefs, substance use, or emotional
  responses to demean or discredit a person;
- presenting unsupported medical certainty or pressuring others to try an
  intervention;
- repeated bad-faith disruption of review or evidence requirements;
- sharing private records, generated inferences, or identifiable research data
  without authorization.

## Reporting

For non-sensitive concerns, open an issue so the behavior can be addressed
transparently. For sensitive matters, contact the maintainer privately through
GitHub. Do not disclose private information in a public report.
