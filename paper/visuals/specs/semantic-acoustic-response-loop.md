# ANT-FIG-001 - Person-moment-journey response model

## Scientific purpose

Introduce the inspectability chain without presenting a causal or therapeutic mechanism.

## Required content

Show semantic intent, acoustic realization, exposure-linked response, and a separately labeled later advisory update.

## Evidence and claim boundary

Governed by ANT-HYP-001, ANT-CLM-001, and ANT-CLM-003. Every transition is proposed; no arrow means efficacy, state change, or automatic learning.

## Source plan

Generate the deterministic SVG with
`scripts/generate_publication_figures.py` while preserving the stable slug,
label, ordered record chain, and separately styled future-advisory return path.

## Accessibility plan

Use ordered boxes, arrow labels, high contrast, grayscale-safe boundaries, canonical alt text, and the manifest long description.

## Failure conditions

Reject a final asset that hides exposure, merges intended and felt state,
removes the hypothesis boundary, implies automatic learning, or retains a
provisional marker.
