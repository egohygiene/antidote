# ANT-TBL-001 - Evidence and claim classification

## Scientific purpose

Define the five non-interchangeable evidence classes before substantive claims appear.

## Required content

Include source, hypothesis, observation, interpretation, claim, supporting artifact, and promotion boundary columns.

## Evidence and claim boundary

Governed by ANT-CLM-003 and ANT-NEG-004. Do not imply that a generated explanation, fixture, or experience promotes itself.

## Source plan

Render a deterministic LaTeX table from EPISTEMOLOGY.md and the claim ledger after issue #37 fixes its narrative placement.

## Accessibility plan

Use explicit row headers, short cells, no color-only encoding, and a linear reading order suitable for HTML.

## Failure conditions

Reject merged classes, ambiguous promotion rules, unsupported examples, or a table too dense for 6.5 inch print width.
