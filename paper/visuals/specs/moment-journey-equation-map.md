# ANT-FIG-009 — Moment, journey, and decision equation map

## Scientific purpose

Make the mathematical spine navigable while keeping definitions, conceptual
hypotheses, operational quantities, estimators, and future empirical models
visibly distinct.

## Required content

- Person–moment state and desired trajectory.
- Candidate acoustic journey and predicted response.
- Safety-, uncertainty-, and agency-constrained selection.
- Independent semantic and waveform continuity quantities.
- Exposure-linked response and human-approved later update.
- A visible epistemic-class label beside every equation family.

## Evidence and claim boundary

The figure may explain proposed relationships. It must not imply an identified
causal model, validated affect predictor, optimal policy, therapeutic benefit,
or neural mechanism. Every symbol must resolve to the notation appendix.

## Source plan

Issue #39 owns the mathematical source and issue #47 owns whole-paper
reconciliation. Produce the final diagram as deterministic SVG from reviewed
notation and equation text; issue #46 owns visual production.

## Accessibility plan

Use shape and text labels in addition to color. Preserve a linear reading path,
minimum print-size labels, descriptive caption, alt text, and a long description
that names equation classes and connections.

## Failure conditions

- An equation changes without updating the figure and glossary.
- A conceptual expression is presented as fitted or empirically validated.
- Symbols are undefined or used inconsistently.
- Arrows imply causality not established by evidence.
