# ANT-FIG-004 - Semantic Intent Mixer and human control surface

## Scientific purpose

Explain typed semantic controls as correctable inputs rather than emotion guarantees.

## Required content

Show mixin purpose, value, neutral position, bounds, exclusions, priority, adapter mapping, conditioning state, and approval.

## Evidence and claim boundary

Governed by ANT-HYP-002 and ANT-CLM-001. A semantic mixin is not a raw prompt fragment or a direct affect control.

## Source plan

Author deterministic SVG from frozen terminology and the accepted generation-specification boundary.

## Accessibility plan

Give every control a textual value and state, use a predictable reading order, and explain the combined conditioning state in prose.

## Failure conditions

Reject unlabeled sliders, irreversible mutations, hidden prompt concatenation, or arrows that claim felt-state causation.
