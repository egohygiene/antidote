# ANT-TBL-003 - Measurement and evidence matrix

## Scientific purpose

Map each non-interchangeable measurement layer to its instrument, timing, provenance, missingness, and permitted interpretation.

## Required content

Include intended controls, acoustic realization, exposure, perceived expression, felt response, usefulness or harm, aftereffect, and out-of-scope clinical outcome.

## Evidence and claim boundary

Governed by ANT-HYP-003 and ANT-CLM-003. Measurement separation does not establish validity or remove error.

Generate deterministic LaTeX from the frozen issue #40 protocol metadata when available.

## Accessibility plan

Use row headers, concise definitions, repeated page headers, and a responsive web projection with no color-only status.

## Failure conditions

Reject merged response constructs, unspecified timing, missing provenance, or clinical outcome inferred from feasibility measures.
