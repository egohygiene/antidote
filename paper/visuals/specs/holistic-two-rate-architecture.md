# ANT-FIG-002 - Holistic two-rate closed-loop architecture

## Scientific purpose

Separate uncertain deliberation and human authority from deterministic audio deadlines.

## Required content

Show one numbered path through consented context, correctable state belief,
semantic intent, journey planning, human approval, an immutable generation
specification, model adapter, generation and verification, future-audio buffer,
deterministic renderer, exposure, and response. Distinguish variable-latency
generation from the fast audio loop: the model worker is not an audio callback,
and only verified buffered material reaches deterministic playback deadlines.

Show cancel, approved-fallback, and immediate-stop paths without routing stop
through deliberation or smoothing. A response may inform only a future
human-reviewed proposal. Show that the worker receives the approved
specification only, that no automatic learning path exists, and that provenance
records lineage without asserting truth or benefit.

## Evidence and claim boundary

Governed by ANT-SRC-002, ANT-HYP-002, ANT-OBS-002, ANT-CLM-003,
ANT-CLM-004, ANT-CLM-005, ANT-NEG-003, and ANT-NEG-004. Label proposed,
executable synthetic, and mock-to-target elements redundantly and never imply a
validated controller, hard-real-time model, autonomous learning, or human
outcome.

## Source plan

Author structured SVG from the architecture evidence map, System Design status
inventory, model-worker contract, and accepted authority decisions; keep every
label and edge reviewable in source. The repository SVG is a deterministic
redraw of an author-approved AI-assisted concept. It embeds no generated raster,
external image, script, or remote resource.

The executable synthetic slice includes consent/context review, the Level-1
journey plan, approval and immutable specification, deterministic mock-worker
generation and file verification, deliberate synthetic playback state, and a
response-record path. No qualifying human exposure or response is observed.
Probabilistic state belief, the generalized Semantic Intent Mixer, future-audio
queue, adaptive continuity renderer, and real model path remain proposed.
Adapter, generation, listening/exposure, and response cards must expose their
mixed current-record/target-observation status without collapsing generated
artifact, exposure, and response.

## Accessibility plan

Use two vertically separated regions, numbered stages 1 through 12, redundant
solid/dashed/split status styles, and a long description that preserves
cross-lane authority. Use exactly one gold approval handoff from human approval
through the immutable specification to the model adapter. Every directed edge
has one arrowhead. Text, line styles, boundaries, and status words must remain
legible in PDF, responsive HTML, grayscale, and common color-vision-deficiency
views; color alone never communicates implementation state.

## Failure conditions

Reject hidden adaptation, worker-owned personal history, a direct
response-to-state update, duplicated approval arrows, missing cancel/stop/fallback
paths, an unqualified provenance claim, or any implication that the generator
runs in the audio callback. Reject hard-real-time generation, seamless-playback,
validated-controller, therapeutic, and human-outcome claims.
