\section{System design}
\label{sec:system-design}

The proposed system is organized as an interpretable loop rather than an
opaque optimization target. Semantic intent describes the state transition a
person is seeking, the generator produces an acoustic realization, and the
research record preserves response alongside the exact controls and artifact
identity. Feedback may inform a later trial only under a versioned protocol.
The holistic architecture, authority boundaries, and two-rate timing model are
allocated in the governed visual manifest for production alongside this
section rather than implied by the simpler introductory loop.

\subsection{Conceptual conditional model}
\label{sec:conceptual-conditional-model}

Let $A$ denote generated audio, $S$ the current state, $T$ the desired state or
trajectory, $H$ the individual's prior response history, and $C$ the semantic
and creative control context. The working model considers
\begin{equation}
  P(A \mid S, T, H, C).
  \label{eq:conditional-audio}
\end{equation}
After exposure at trial $t$, audio $A_t$ is associated with measured response
$R_t$. The history may then be updated with the tuple
$(S_t, T_t, C_t, A_t, R_t)$. Equation~\ref{eq:conditional-audio} is a conceptual
model, not an identified statistical model or demonstrated mechanism.
