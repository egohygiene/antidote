\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Antidote asks how a local research instrument can represent, generate, and
evaluate an audio journey for one person in one moment without collapsing the
records that give the journey its meaning or evidentiary status. At the design
level, RQ1 is answered by treating consented source context, a reviewed working
projection, explicit semantic intent, an accepted time-indexed plan,
adapter-specific conditioning, measured acoustic realization, actual exposure,
separated response observations, and any later advisory update as distinct,
versioned, provenance-linked objects. Human approval, correction, interruption,
and stop authority remain explicit transitions throughout that chain. This
answer specifies what an inspectable instrument must preserve; it does not show
that the instrument correctly understands a person or produces a useful,
continuous, safe, or beneficial experience.

The manuscript makes five bounded contributions at different evidence levels.
It situates the problem among prior personalized, current-to-target,
closed-loop, controllable, physiological, and longitudinal music systems rather
than claiming those ingredients as new. It formalizes the end-to-end
inspectability contract and the non-interchangeability of intended controls,
realized acoustics, perceived expression, felt response, immediate usefulness
or harm, and later aftereffect. It proposes a two-rate architecture in which
person-correctable deliberation, typed semantic mixins, short-horizon planning,
and variable-latency generation remain outside deterministic playback and stop
deadlines. It defines a local-first human-authority model, with only a bounded
synthetic path presently implemented. Finally, it freezes a prospective staged
feasibility and reporting protocol that preserves null, negative, missing,
interrupted, burdensome, and adverse records rather than manufacturing a
favorable Results narrative.

RQ2 and RQ3 remain unanswered. No qualifying real-model technical package or
formal human-study package exists, and collection authority remains false. RQ2
requires promoted synthetic conformance evidence followed by a frozen
real-model, adapter, analyzer, hardware, timing, continuity, integrity, and
recovery qualification. The record-feasibility prerequisite to RQ3 additionally
requires privacy, consent, independent-review, assignment, instrument,
analysis, and pilot stop/go gates. A useful advisory relationship would then
require a later prospectively frozen evaluation on new within-person records;
it cannot be inferred from the planned H1 series. Antidote's central proposition
is therefore not that a system knows what a person needs, but that a person can
remain the authority over a moment-specific audio hypothesis whose
transformations are visible enough to inspect, challenge, and study.
