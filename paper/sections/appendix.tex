\section{Consent and context-projection record}
\label{app:consent-context-record}

Any future qualifying session record must make the consent boundary auditable
without placing private content in this public appendix. The protected record
must identify the protocol and source revision, decision point, separately
authorized action, purpose, consent-grant revision, permitted source types and
fields, explicit exclusions, retention mode, expiry or revocation state, and
the person who reviewed the grant. Inspect, project, generate, analyze, play,
retain, learn, and export remain separate authorizations; one action cannot be
inferred from another.

The protected context-projection record must identify every contributing source
event, semantic item, derivation rule and version, uncertainty, conflict,
omission, person edit, and final review disposition. It must preserve a digest
and provenance for the exact approved projection while keeping raw private text
and row-level identifiers outside public artifacts. The model-worker record must
show that only the approved semantic projection, accepted journey-plan revision,
and immutable generation specification crossed the process boundary. A later
correction, revocation, deletion, or expiry must create an attributable event
and invalidate affected derived projections rather than rewrite the original
record silently.

Public reporting may expose only the privacy-reviewed aggregate envelope and
the minimum revision and integrity metadata approved for that destination. The
private record must retain the export decision, redactions, excluded fields,
reviewer disposition, and the effect of withdrawal or deletion on payloads,
projections, analyses, backups, and previously shared artifacts. This checklist
specifies what must be recorded; it does not establish meaningful consent,
privacy, secure deletion, or current collection authority.
