\section{Consent and context-projection record}
\label{app:consent-context-record}

Any future qualifying session record must make the consent boundary auditable
without placing private content in this public appendix. The protected record
must identify the protocol and source revision, decision point, separately
authorized action, purpose, consent-grant revision, permitted source types and
fields, explicit exclusions, retention mode, expiry or revocation state, and
the person who reviewed the grant. Inspect, project, generate, analyze, play,
retain, learn, and export remain separate authorizations; one action cannot be
inferred from another.

The protected context-projection record must identify every contributing source
event, semantic item, derivation rule and version, uncertainty, conflict,
omission, person edit, and final review disposition. It must preserve a digest
and provenance for the exact approved projection while keeping raw private text
and row-level identifiers outside public artifacts. The model-worker record must
show that only the approved semantic projection, accepted journey-plan revision,
and immutable generation specification crossed the process boundary. A later
correction, revocation, deletion, or expiry must create an attributable event
and invalidate affected derived projections rather than rewrite the original
record silently.

Public reporting may expose only the privacy-reviewed aggregate envelope and
the minimum revision and integrity metadata approved for that destination. The
private record must retain the export decision, redactions, excluded fields,
reviewer disposition, and the effect of withdrawal or deletion on payloads,
projections, analyses, backups, and previously shared artifacts. This checklist
specifies what must be recorded; it does not establish meaningful consent,
privacy, secure deletion, or current collection authority.

\section{Provenance and reproducibility checklist}
\label{app:provenance-reproducibility}

The checklist below states the minimum record needed to reproduce or audit one
reported Antidote artifact. A field marked unavailable remains unavailable; it
must not be inferred from a neighboring record or replaced with a zero.

\begin{description}
  \item[Source and build identity.] Record the full Git revision, paper version
    and stage, build theme, source-date epoch, operating system, build commands,
    and the versions or immutable commits named by
    \texttt{dependencies/beacon.lock.toml},
    \texttt{publication/antidote-site-suite.lock.json}, language toolchain
    files, and package lockfiles. Retain \texttt{provenance.json},
    \texttt{publication.json}, \texttt{site.json}, and
    \texttt{SHA256SUMS} with the generated PDF, HTML, and source archive.

  \item[Protocol and reporting identity.] Name protocol
    \texttt{ANT-PROT-FEAS-001}, its semantic version, content-lock digest,
    freeze date, deviation log, collection-authority state, and the exact
    \texttt{ANT-REPORT-RESULTS-001} version. Register every source package and
    promotion decision; an empty registry is an unavailable result state, not
    evidence of no effect.

  \item[Model and artifact identity.] For a synthetic record, identify the
    deterministic mock worker, fixture, schema version, seed, request, output
    digest, warnings, interruption state, and test revision. A future T1 record
    must additionally identify the real model, adapter, weights and licenses,
    hardware profile, analyzers, generation parameters, mutation manifest,
    seed policy, attempted outputs, failures, exclusions, and output-rights
    review. Those T1 records are currently unavailable.

  \item[Exposure and response identity.] A future H1 record must link the
    approved plan and verified artifact to actual playback, stop or
    interruption events, response windows, corrections, missingness reasons,
    adverse observations, and the applicable consent, retention, and review
    decisions. Raw personal content and row-level identifiers remain protected;
    only a separately approved aggregate public envelope may enter a public
    result package. No qualifying H1 exposure or response package exists at
    this revision.

  \item[Claim and exclusion audit.] Record which source, observation, or result
    supports each reported statement; the claim-ledger disposition; unresolved
    contradictions; and every omitted, failed, null, negative, mismatched,
    missing, or adverse record. Confirm that synthetic fixtures are not labeled
    human outcomes and that provenance or checksum agreement is not presented
    as truth, consent, privacy, ownership, safety, or efficacy.
\end{description}
