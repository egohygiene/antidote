\section{Related work}

The living source atlas remains broader than the material this manuscript will
cite. The governed comparison covers music and affect regulation, personalized
music systems, controllable generative audio, adaptive or closed-loop systems,
music as an active setting variable, and semantic interpretability. Individual
claims may enter the manuscript only from their promoted source records.

MindMelody is an important direct comparator because it describes a closed-loop
system that mediates EEG-derived affect through a semantic planning layer and
controllable music generation \citep{zhang2026mindmelody}. Its existence is
evidence that this design neighborhood is active prior art. Its reported pilot
results are not evidence that Antidote is effective. The broader comparator
review also rejects first-system claims for closed-loop, current-to-target,
real-time generative, interpretable, physiologically adaptive, and longitudinal
music systems.

\subsection{Bounded contribution}

Antidote therefore does not claim those components individually as new. It
formalizes a governed combination that preserves consented context,
user-extensible semantic intent, time-indexed planning, measured acoustic
realization, actual exposure, perceived and felt response, usefulness or harm,
aftereffect, provenance, and human-approved within-person updating as distinct
records. The bounded review did not identify that complete chain in one
evaluated system; this is a contribution rationale, not proof of global
novelty, learnability, or benefit.
