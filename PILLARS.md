# Antidote Pillars

## Role

The pillars are enduring capabilities. They are not frameworks, model names,
screens, repositories, or roadmap phases.

## Pillar 1: Interpretable personal sonic language

Antidote must let a person express descriptors, metaphors, memories,
preferences, exclusions, and uncertainty in language they can inspect and
correct. Population-level emotion labels may inform but never replace this
personal meaning layer.

## Pillar 2: Moment-specific acoustic journeys

Antidote must represent a transition through time, not only a target label. A
journey has a starting state, desired direction, stages, constraints, acoustic
controls, duration, and safe exit behavior.

## Pillar 3: Local agency and consent

Antidote must keep authority over context, generation, playback, retention,
adaptation, and export visible to the person. The local source of truth remains
usable without a required remote service.

## Pillar 4: Reproducible evidence

Antidote must connect source context, plan, model, parameters, output, acoustic
features, exposure, response, and interpretation through versioned provenance.
Scientific claims remain downstream of that evidence.

## Pillar 5: Cautious longitudinal learning

Antidote must learn within-person patterns without converting one intense
moment into a permanent rule. Updates remain attributable, uncertain,
reversible, and subordinate to current choice.

## Balance

No pillar is complete in isolation. A moving experience without provenance is
not sufficient research evidence. A reproducible artifact that ignored present
consent is not a successful system. An adaptive model that cannot explain its
inputs or be overridden is outside Antidote's intended architecture.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- **Observed:** The conceptual research loop already contains semantic intent,
  acoustic realization, response capture, and adaptation.
- **Decided for the prototype:** All five pillars receive an explicit boundary
  even when their implementation is initially minimal.
- **Proposed:** Health metrics and synchronized sensors may later extend the
  evidence pillar through optional adapters.
- **Open question:** Which minimal response instruments best preserve emotional
  nuance without making the first session burdensome?
