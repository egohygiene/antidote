Working model

Intent becomes structure. Structure meets response.

The research frame keeps each translation inspectable and preserves uncertainty at every boundary.

  1. 01

    Semantic direction

    A person describes a desired state or trajectory through an inspectable, extensible vocabulary.

  2. 02

    Generated structure

    The system records its model, controls, output identity, checksum, and measurable realization.

  3. 03

    Individual observation

    Subjective and optional auxiliary measures remain distinct from interpretation and later adaptation.

Scientific boundary

The scaffold is real. The evidence is not here yet.

The originating experience is hypothesis-generating context, not proof of treatment efficacy or a mechanism.

This draft is

  • A research question and candidate system model.
  • A structure for literature synthesis and protocol design.
  • A reproducible PDF, accessible web edition, provenance record, and source package.

This draft is not

  • Evidence of clinical efficacy or a neurological mechanism.
  • A frozen protocol or completed participant study.
  • A substitute for professional care or independent ethics review.
