Working model

One interpretable loop, with uncertainty preserved at every boundary.

01 / intent

Semantic direction

A person describes a desired state or trajectory using a vocabulary that remains inspectable and extensible.

02 / realization

Generated structure

The system records the model, controls, output identity, checksum, and measurable acoustic realization.

03 / response

Individual observation

Subjective and optional auxiliary measures remain distinct from interpretation, claims, and future adaptations.

Scientific boundary

The scaffold is complete. The evidence is not.

What this draft is

  • A research question and candidate system model.
  • A structure for literature synthesis and protocol design.
  • A reproducible PDF, accessible web, provenance, and source package.

What this draft is not

  • Evidence of treatment efficacy or a neurological mechanism.
  • A frozen protocol or completed participant study.
  • A substitute for professional care or independent ethics review.
