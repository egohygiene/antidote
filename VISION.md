# Antidote Vision

## Vision statement

A person can create an audio journey that is intelligible, private,
moment-specific, and responsive to their own lived meanings—then inspect why it
was created, what it contained, how they responded, and what the system learned.

## Desired future state

- A person names their current state, desired transition, time horizon,
  inclusions, exclusions, and personally meaningful sonic language.
- The system produces a human-readable journey plan before it generates audio.
- Each generated artifact remains traceable to approved context, a model
  revision, parameters, a seed, an acoustic analysis, and a response record.
- Personalization learns within one person's longitudinal history without
  pretending that population averages or a diagnosis define that person.
- The experience remains useful offline and locally owned; optional
  organization or cloud services never become the only holder of portable
  state.
- Research outputs distinguish technical feasibility, acoustic adherence,
  perceived emotion, felt response, usefulness, harm, and clinical outcomes.

## Intended transformation

Antidote moves adaptive audio from opaque one-shot prompting toward an
interpretable loop:

> current moment → desired trajectory → semantic intent → acoustic plan →
> generated audio → lived response → cautious personal learning

## Anti-vision

Antidote is not an automated therapist, a universal emotion-to-frequency map, a
brain-state controller, a streaming engagement engine, or a system that mines a
person's private writing and changes their experience without explicit,
current consent.

## Directional signals

- A participant can explain and edit the planned journey before generation.
- A researcher can reproduce or audit a run from non-sensitive approved inputs.
- A model adapter can be replaced without changing Antidote's domain language.
- A failed or uncomfortable session can be stopped, recorded, and excluded from
  future suggestions without blame.
- The prototype produces evidence useful to the paper without overstating what
  a single-user study establishes.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- **Observed:** Controllable local music-generation systems and established
  repeated-measures methods make a transparent prototype technically plausible.
- **Decided for the prototype:** User-visible intent and provenance take
  precedence over invisible optimization.
- **Proposed:** Carefully bounded adaptive studies may follow descriptive
  N-of-1 work.
- **Open question:** Which controls are reliable enough across models and
  hardware to support a stable journey language?
