# Antidote Design

## Design philosophy

Antidote should feel intimate without pretending to know the person, scientific
without becoming clinical, and imaginative without obscuring what the system is
doing. The experience should reduce cognitive load during a vulnerable moment
while keeping evidence and advanced controls reachable.

## Intended qualities

- **Grounded:** Begin with what the person says is true now.
- **Personal:** Use their language and meanings without turning them into fixed
  traits.
- **Legible:** Show context, plan, controls, model, uncertainty, and changes at
  the level the person chooses.
- **Agency-preserving:** Preview, edit, cancel, stop, exclude, retry, and delete
  remain available.
- **Gentle:** Avoid pressure, gamification, streaks, blame, or emotional
  intensity as a success signal.
- **Evidence-aware:** Separate intention, realization, experience, and later
  interpretation.
- **Accessible:** Support keyboard and assistive technology, reduced motion,
  readable contrast, plain language, and alternatives to waveform-only meaning.

## Core journey

1. **Arrive:** choose a new session or revisit an earlier artifact without
   automatically starting playback.
2. **Check in:** describe the current state, desired direction, duration, and
   anything that should not appear.
3. **Choose context:** select exact sources and see what will be derived.
4. **Shape language:** add descriptors, metaphors, textures, memories,
   instruments, and constraints.
5. **Review the journey:** inspect stages and acoustic controls in human
   language; edit or regenerate the plan.
6. **Generate:** see model, estimated resources, progress, downgrade warnings,
   and cancel behavior.
7. **Listen:** start deliberately; keep stop, pause, intensity, and skip controls
   visible; avoid distracting data collection during the experience.
8. **Respond:** record felt response, resonance, mismatch, helpfulness, harm,
   notes, and whether later follow-up is welcome.
9. **Learn:** review any proposed personal mapping update and accept, edit,
   reject, or forget it.

## Emotional safety experience

- A person can begin without providing sensitive context.
- Exclusions and sensory constraints appear before positive preferences.
- Playback does not auto-start after generation.
- Stop is persistent, unambiguous, and does not require confirmation.
- A difficult response opens grounding and record options without claiming to
  provide crisis or clinical care.
- Follow-up is optional and never framed as a failure to complete the journey.

## Failure experience

Failures state what happened, what did not happen, whether any partial artifact
exists, what personal context reached which boundary, and the smallest recovery
step. A model's failure to follow a control is described as system uncertainty,
not user error.

## Research and advanced views

The default interface uses plain-language stages and controls. An expandable
research view reveals identifiers, schemas, parameters, seeds, hashes, feature
reports, provenance, and protocol assignment. Neither view changes canonical
state independently.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- **Observed:** The conceptual flow and research paper already define the main
  semantic–acoustic–response sequence.
- **Decided for the prototype:** The inspectable journey plan is a first-class
  screen, not a hidden prompt.
- **Proposed:** A magazine-like “insight page” mode may later explain each
  session without exposing sensitive source text.
- **Open question:** How much acoustic detail can remain understandable without
  interrupting emotional presence?
