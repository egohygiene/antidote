# Scientific Boundaries and Epistemic Rules

## Core rule

The system should be imaginative about **generation** and conservative about **interpretation**.

## Evidence classes

```text
SOURCE
What an external source actually establishes.

HYPOTHESIS
What the project proposes could be true.

OBSERVATION
What occurred in an experiment or N-of-1 case.

INTERPRETATION
What the researchers think an observation may mean.

CLAIM
What the manuscript ultimately states as supported.
```

These should never silently collapse into one another.

## N-of-1 origin

The originating catharsis experience is valuable as an observation and hypothesis generator. It is not evidence of efficacy, mechanism, or generalizability.

## Neuroscience

Avoid simple deterministic mappings such as `diagnosis X → brain structure Y → sound frequency Z`. Complex mental-health conditions do not generally reduce to one brain structure or one audio parameter.

## Binaural beats

Treat binaural beats and related auditory phenomena as candidate stimulus primitives whose effects can be tested. Do not encode simplistic rules such as "frequency X causes mental state Y" unless a specific claim is strongly supported by evidence.

## Spiritual and contemplative models

Traditional systems such as chakras should not be presented as established neuroanatomical structures. They can instead be modeled as human meaning systems or phenomenological ontologies whose relationships to other representations can be investigated.

## Clinical language

Early work should avoid claims that the system treats a disorder, cures symptoms, produces a specific neurological state, replaces therapy or medication, or has established clinical efficacy. A first paper is better framed as a methods, system, HCI, or N-of-1 feasibility study.

## Reproducibility

Preserve model identity/version, prompts and semantic controls, generation settings, output identity/checksum, acoustic features, baseline and target states, response measures, timestamps, protocol version, and mutations between trials wherever possible.
