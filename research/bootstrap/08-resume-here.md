## Current state

The central question is:

> Can an adaptive system learn an individual mapping from interpretable sonic language → generated acoustic structure → affective response, and use that mapping to better target future state transitions?

The origin is an N-of-1 cathartic creative experience, preserved as hypothesis-generating rather than proof. The strongest likely novelty is the longitudinal mapping between interpretable semantic controls, actual measured acoustic realization, and individual response.

The concept of **probability-space sculpting** is a computational metaphor/model for how semantic prompts constrain generative audio distributions.

Psychedelic/ketamine music research, music psychology, generative AI, neuroscience/psychophysiology, and phenomenology/human meaning systems are the five primary literature branches.

## Important boundaries

- Do not frame the N-of-1 observation as efficacy evidence.
- Do not equate traditional systems with neuroanatomy.
- Treat binaural beats as testable primitives with uncertain effects.
- Do not reduce mental-health states to simplistic brain-region mappings.
- Keep sources, hypotheses, observations, interpretations, and claims explicitly separate.
- Preserve provenance for prompts, generations, analyses, protocols, and responses.
