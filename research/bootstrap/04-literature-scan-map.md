# Literature Scan Map

This is a roadmap for the deep literature scan. It is not itself a bibliography; every eventual citation should be verified from primary sources.

## Stream 1 — Music psychology / affect regulation

Investigate music-induced emotion, emotion regulation through music, the iso principle, preference and familiarity, individual differences, sequencing and trajectories, and active versus passive listening.

## Stream 2 — Generative AI / controllable audio

Investigate text-to-music/audio generation, semantic conditioning, embeddings and latent controls, controllability and prompt sensitivity, acoustic-feature realization of semantic prompts, personalization, and evaluation of generated audio.

## Stream 3 — Neuroscience / psychophysiology

Investigate music-evoked affect and reward, autonomic responses, interoception, EEG and neural entrainment where appropriate, HRV/EDA/respiration measures, predictive processing, and individual variability. Avoid simplistic brain-region mappings.

## Stream 4 — Psychedelic and ketamine research

Investigate music in psychedelic-assisted therapy, ketamine/esketamine + music, set and setting, music selection protocols, emotional breakthrough, mystical/peak experiences, integration, and personalization. Treat this as a strong adjacent experiential literature rather than prematurely making the project a psychedelic-treatment system.

## Stream 5 — Human meaning systems / phenomenology

Investigate phenomenology of music, embodied cognition, interoception, metaphor and conceptual mapping, personal semantic vocabularies, contemplative traditions, dimensional versus categorical representations of experience, and ontology alignment.

## Adjacent systems and novelty threats

Deep-scan systems that already combine state sensing → intervention selection → generative music → response feedback → adaptation. `MindMelody` is an important novelty comparator and should be examined closely from its primary paper and implementation, alongside similar systems found later.

## Literature matrix fields

Record citation, research question, population, study design, stimulus, state representation, music representation, signals, adaptation mechanism, personalization mechanism, outcomes, findings, limitations, relationship to this project, novelty overlap, and evidence quality.
