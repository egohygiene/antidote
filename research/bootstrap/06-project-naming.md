# Project Naming Notes

The research project name is not fixed.

## Current candidates

### Antidote

Memorable and emotionally resonant, but semantically implies treatment or cure. A good compromise is to use `antidote` as a provisional project/prototype identity while keeping academic titles conservative and descriptive.

### Attune

Evokes personalization, alignment, musical tuning, and iterative calibration with less clinical loading.

### Undertow

Distinctive and organically derived from the project's sonic vocabulary; captures the idea of an underlying emotional trajectory.

Other explored directions include Resonance, Serum, Entrain, Echo, Accord, Tuning, and Sonora.

## Suggested naming model

```text
Beacon
└── reusable research-authoring / project-bootstrapping platform

Antidote / Attune / Undertow
└── adaptive-generative-audio research program + prototype

Academic paper
└── conservative descriptive title
```
