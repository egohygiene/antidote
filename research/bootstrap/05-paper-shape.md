# Candidate First Paper Shape

## Recommended scope

The first paper should probably be a **methods + system + feasibility / N-of-1 paper**, not a clinical efficacy or neuroscience-mechanism paper.

A tentative descriptive title is:

> An Interpretable Semantic Framework for Personalized Adaptive Generative Audio: Design and N-of-1 Feasibility

The final title should be chosen only after the deep literature and novelty scan.

## Candidate manuscript structure

```text
Abstract
1. Introduction
2. Background
   2.1 Music and affect regulation
   2.2 Personalized music systems
   2.3 Generative music and controllability
   2.4 Adaptive / closed-loop music systems
   2.5 Psychedelic and ketamine music research
   2.6 Semantic control and interpretability
3. Research Gap
4. Semantic Closed-Loop Framework
5. N-of-1 Feasibility Method
6. Prototype / Research Tooling
7. Analysis Strategy
8. Limitations
9. Future Multi-Participant Study
10. Conclusion
```

## What Paper #1 does not require

A clinical trial, EEG or fMRI, proving neurological mechanisms, a large cohort, training a foundation audio model, proving treatment efficacy, or asserting universal psychological meanings for acoustic features.

## Visual / publication identity

The paper can be visually polished while remaining academically legible. Potential figures include a project mark, probability-space model, state-transition loop, semantic → acoustic → response map, protocol timeline, prompt mutation lineage, waveform/spectrogram examples, literature landscape, and evidence/ontology maps. The principle is **information design, not decoration**.
