# Living source atlas

This directory preserves Antidote's broad research-discovery layer. It is
deliberately larger than both `paper/references.bib` and the citations that
appear in the manuscript.

## Canonical artifacts

- [`literature-voyage-v0.1.md`](literature-voyage-v0.1.md) is the complete
  2026-08-27 narrative checkpoint. It is preserved as a historical research
  snapshot; later corrections belong in source records and the catalog rather
  than silent rewrites of that checkpoint.
- [`../sources/catalog.json`](../sources/catalog.json) is the current
  machine-readable ownership and status view.
- [`../sources/README.md`](../sources/README.md) defines source states and
  promotion gates.
- [`../../paper/references.bib`](../../paper/references.bib) is the verified
  bibliography shelf. An entry may remain intentionally uncited.

## Reading order

1. Read the voyage's **Initial conclusions** and **Claim-strength matrix** to
   understand the evidence boundary.
2. Read the ten-item **Highest-priority reading voyage** before drafting
   Related Work.
3. Review the **Closest prior systems and novelty map** before making a
   contribution or novelty statement.
4. Use the domain clusters for targeted section work.
5. Consult the architecture cluster before changing the prototype rationale;
   implementation documentation does not establish scientific benefit.
6. Use the catalog and source records—not the historical voyage—as the current
   authority for promotion and manuscript use.

The atlas may preserve contradictory, superseded, rejected, or background-only
material. Presence means “worth remembering,” not “endorsed” or “supported.”
