## Source claims assessed

- POMDPs formalize sequential action selection when the underlying state is not
  directly observed.
- Available history is summarized as a probability distribution over states,
  commonly called a belief state.
- Exact solution is computationally difficult and approximate methods introduce
  additional assumptions.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The formalism does not supply valid Antidote states, observations, actions,
  transition probabilities, rewards, or ethical constraints.
- A probability distribution can still be misspecified or poorly calibrated.
- Autonomous POMDP optimization remains outside v0.

## Allowed manuscript use

Use as the formal anchor for retaining alternatives and uncertainty rather than
collapsing observations into one asserted emotional state.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not claim Antidote implements a POMDP, infers a true hidden emotion, or has a
validated optimal policy.
