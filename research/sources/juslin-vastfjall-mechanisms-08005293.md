## Source claims assessed

- The theoretical framework argues that music-evoked emotion cannot be
  interpreted without considering how it was induced.
- In addition to cognitive appraisal, it describes brain-stem reflexes,
  evaluative conditioning, emotional contagion, visual imagery, episodic
  memory, and musical expectancy as candidate induction mechanisms.
- The mechanisms differ in timing, volitional influence, cultural dependence,
  information focus, and dependence on musical structure.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- This is a theoretical synthesis, not an evaluation of Antidote, a generator,
  or a therapeutic intervention.
- The framework does not imply that a measured acoustic feature identifies the
  mechanism operating for a particular listener.
- Later empirical work and later versions of the framework may refine the
  proposed mechanisms.

## Allowed manuscript use

Cite to justify treating musical response as multiply determined and
context-dependent rather than inferring a universal emotion from one acoustic
feature or model label.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not present any mechanism as a deterministic brain mapping, diagnose a
listener from music response, or infer efficacy from theoretical plausibility.
