## Source claims assessed

- The prototype continuously generates controllable synthesized affective music
  in real time inside a user-calibrated EEG feedback loop.
- The music engine supports smooth parameter evolution rather than abrupt track
  switching, with its online control updated at subsecond intervals.
- A listener study included 11 participants; a separate proof-of-concept BCI
  pilot included five participants.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The BCI evaluation is explicitly preliminary, very small, and participant
  results are heterogeneous.
- Self-induced emotion and EEG classification are not objective ground truth for
  therapeutic need or benefit.
- The paper does not report Antidote's proposed contextual consent projection,
  user-authored semantic language, or longitudinal provenance chain.

## Allowed manuscript use

Cite as peer-reviewed precedent for continuous, seamless, real-time affective
music generation in a person-calibrated physiological feedback loop.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not claim that continuous affective feedback, personalized calibration, or
smooth real-time musical transitions originate with Antidote. Do not generalize
the five-person pilot to efficacy.
