## Source claims assessed

- Interactive machine learning makes the end user part of model development
  and correction rather than only a consumer of a final prediction.
- The authors argue for involving users during exploration and refinement and
  document interaction failures as well as opportunities.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The paper spans multiple systems and is not an evaluation of therapeutic
  audio, low-cognitive-load controls, or emotionally intense sessions.
- User feedback can itself be inconsistent, burdensome, or misunderstood.

## Allowed manuscript use

Use to ground editable hypotheses, user correction, direct manipulation, and
evaluation of the entire human–system loop.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not claim that more feedback, more knobs, or human involvement necessarily
improves accuracy, trust, well-being, or safety.
