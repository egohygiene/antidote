## Source claims assessed

- iHeartLift is an early closed-loop biofeedback system using heart-rate
  variability and music-tempo variation.
- It adapts playback from an existing music set rather than generating a
  semantically planned acoustic journey.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The short conference report provides limited evidence for generalization,
  mechanism, durability, or clinical outcome.
- Its tempo-focused selection strategy does not represent the full Antidote
  semantic or provenance model.

## Allowed manuscript use

Cite as early engineering precedent for real-time physiological feedback linked
to a controllable music parameter.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not claim that physiological closed-loop music control originates with
Antidote or that iHeartLift validates therapeutic benefit.
