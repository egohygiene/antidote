## Source claims assessed

- CENT extends CONSORT reporting guidance to individual N-of-1 trials and
  series of N-of-1 trials.
- It emphasizes transparent reporting of the design, periods, assignments,
  participant flow, outcomes, harms, and analyses needed to appraise a study.
- It complements prospective protocol guidance; it does not retroactively make
  an incompletely specified study reproducible.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- CENT is a reporting checklist, not evidence that a study design is valid or
  an intervention is effective.
- Its clinical-trial vocabulary does not automatically apply to an exploratory
  audio research instrument.
- The current design/protocol manuscript has no formal results to report under
  CENT.

## Allowed manuscript use

Preserve on the verified bibliography shelf for the Results and reporting gate,
where it may support complete accounting of assignment, exposure, missingness,
withdrawal, adverse events, and analysis deviations.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not cite CENT as evidence of Antidote feasibility, benefit, safety, ethics
approval, or successful protocol execution.
