## Source claims assessed

- The review covers 51 studies connecting smartphone or wearable measures with
  depression-related variables.
- It identifies small samples, short follow-up, inconsistent reporting,
  feature-construction variability, and missing-data handling as threats to
  reproducibility and generalizability.
- Reported associations and predictive models are best treated as
  hypothesis-generating given those limitations.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The review concerns depression monitoring, not moment-specific music response.
- It does not evaluate Antidote's proposed signals or architecture.
- A review of limitations cannot establish that every future passive measure is
  unusable; it establishes a high validation burden.

## Allowed manuscript use

Use as qualifying evidence that passive signals are incomplete, missingness is
informative and dangerous to ignore, and external validity must be tested.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not claim that vitals or phone behavior objectively reveal mood, need,
diagnosis, readiness, or the correct audio response.
