## Source claims assessed

- EBU R 128 recommends programme-loudness normalization at -23 LUFS and use of
  Loudness Range and Maximum True Peak Level descriptors.
- The recommendation distinguishes loudness measurement from simple peak
  normalization and supplies a common broadcast reference.
- It permits additional momentary and short-term descriptors for
  characterization and production needs.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The recommendation targets programme exchange and broadcast practice, not
  personalized headphone listening or therapeutic exposure.
- It does not define musical-boundary compatibility, affective continuity, a
  comfortable listening level, or an individual hearing-safety threshold.
- Antidote does not yet implement or claim EBU R 128 conformance.

## Allowed manuscript use

Use as a normative measurement anchor for loudness and true-peak features
within a future acoustic verification layer.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not present -23 LUFS as a universal listening target, personalized dose,
hearing-safety limit, seamless-transition rule, or current implementation.
