## Source claims assessed

- The systematic review included 14 EEG studies of binaural-beat stimulation
  and the brainwave-entrainment hypothesis.
- Five studies reported results consistent with entrainment, eight reported
  contradictory results, and one reported mixed results.
- Stimulation procedures, experimental designs, EEG measures, and analyses
  were sufficiently heterogeneous to limit comparison across studies.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The review addresses oscillatory EEG evidence rather than establishing a
  behavioral, therapeutic, or person-specific effect.
- Inconsistency does not establish that entrainment is impossible; it leaves
  the mechanism unresolved and identifies a need for standardized studies.
- The included studies do not justify a universal mapping from a beat
  frequency to a mental state or outcome.

## Allowed manuscript use

Cite as systematic-review evidence that binaural-beat EEG entrainment findings
are inconsistent and methodologically heterogeneous. Use it to separate an
unresolved neural mechanism from behavioral outcomes reported elsewhere.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not claim that binaural beats reliably entrain the brain, cannot affect any
listener, treat a condition, or produce a specified psychological state.
