## Source claims assessed

- SPENT extends general protocol guidance with items specific to prospective
  N-of-1 trials.
- Its protocol concerns include the intervention sequence, allocation,
  randomization, blinding, period and washout decisions, outcomes, analysis,
  adverse events, participant involvement, and protocol changes.
- The checklist is designed to improve protocol completeness and transparency;
  it is not a substitute for scientific, statistical, or ethics review.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- SPENT was developed for N-of-1 trials, commonly in health and treatment
  settings. Antidote's conditions are research-instrument configurations rather
  than validated treatments.
- Checklist conformance does not identify a causal estimand, validate a measure,
  eliminate carryover, or determine whether a self-study requires institutional
  review.
- The Antidote protocol deliberately stops short of a clinical trial claim.

## Allowed manuscript use

Use as the principal reporting framework for prospectively specifying repeated
within-person conditions, periods, assignment, carryover, analysis, adverse
events, and amendments before formal collection.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not cite SPENT as ethics approval, registration, causal identification,
clinical-trial status, safety evidence, or proof that the planned sample and
analysis are sufficient.
