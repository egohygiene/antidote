## Source claims assessed

- The systematic review maps music-neurofeedback methods, including affective
  brain-computer music interfaces relevant to Antidote's comparator set.
- It reports heterogeneous methodologies and no consensus on how
  neurofeedback success should be measured.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- Review-level synthesis does not independently validate every included system
  or establish the effectiveness of generated music.
- Heterogeneous sensing, feedback, and outcome definitions constrain
  comparison.

## Allowed manuscript use

Cite as synthesis evidence that music-in-the-loop research exists but remains
methodologically heterogeneous and lacks a settled success standard.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not use the review as proof of Antidote efficacy, novelty, or a universal
neurofeedback mechanism.
