## Source claims assessed

- The review identified 22 commercial music-based digital therapeutics across
  five categories.
- It concludes that direct evidence for these products remains too limited to
  establish real-world effectiveness or superiority over generic music.
- The review highlights open-label designs, inactive controls, and incomplete
  reporting as recurring evidence risks.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- A scoping review maps the field but does not pool a treatment effect or
  validate any individual product.
- Commercial-system descriptions may be less reproducible than primary
  technical studies.

## Allowed manuscript use

Cite as qualifying field-level evidence that commercial music-based digital
therapeutics exist while product-specific efficacy and comparative advantage
remain under-established.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not call Antidote a digital therapeutic, infer effectiveness from market
presence, or imply that the review establishes generated personalized music as
a treatment.
