## Source claims assessed

- The review searched 884 generative-audio papers and retained 171 full-text
  papers for its negative-impact analysis.
- Sixty-five percent of the analyzed papers discussed a positive potential
  impact, whereas fewer than ten percent discussed any negative potential
  impact.
- The negative-impact themes found in the reviewed literature include
  copyright and training-data concerns, cultural and demographic bias,
  authorship or agency, fraud, deepfakes, privacy, labor, access, and resource
  use. The set is an agenda for review, not an incidence estimate.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The review measures what papers discussed, not the prevalence or severity of
  each harm in deployed systems.
- Its corpus combines music and speech generation; some speech-specific harms
  do not transfer directly to Antidote's intended instrumental-audio scope.
- Copyright, licensing, consent, and ownership conclusions remain dependent on
  the selected model, training-data documentation, output, jurisdiction, and
  intended use.
- The paper does not evaluate Antidote, establish that disclosure mitigates a
  harm, or supply a complete release checklist.

## Allowed manuscript use

Cite as generative-audio-specific evidence that model and dataset review must
include negative impacts, output rights, attribution, cultural bias, agency,
privacy, and foreseeable misuse rather than documenting only expected benefit.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not cite the review as proof that a selected Antidote model infringes rights,
that any generated artifact is original or licensed, that a risk has occurred,
or that the proposed controls resolve the identified concerns.
