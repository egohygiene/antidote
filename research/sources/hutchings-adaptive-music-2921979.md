## Source claims assessed

- The system combines game context and affect representation with multi-agent
  real-time composition.
- It was integrated into two games and evaluated against their original
  soundtracks for immersion and correlation with game-world concepts.
- It demonstrates a neighboring field where music adapts during an unfolding
  interactive experience.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- Gameplay context and immersion are not equivalents of felt need, regulation,
  or benefit.
- The paper does not establish that arbitrary generated audio segments can be
  joined seamlessly.
- Its compositional architecture is not a foundation-model streaming system.

## Allowed manuscript use

Use as peer-reviewed precedent for context-responsive adaptive music and for
separating real-time composition from the event model that drives it.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not claim therapeutic transfer, universal immersion improvement, smooth
waveform interpolation, or equivalence with Antidote's proposed generator.
