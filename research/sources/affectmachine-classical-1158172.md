## Source claims assessed

- The expert system maps continuous valence and arousal controls to an
  interpretable rule-based and probabilistic music generator in real time.
- Musical parameters can change bar by bar; trajectories may be predefined or
  supplied by a future sensor loop.
- A 26-participant study tested whether 39 generated stimuli communicated
  intended affect, not whether they improved health or induced a desired state.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The study measures perceived/expression alignment rather than felt emotion,
  regulation, safety, or clinical outcome.
- Biofeedback integration and listener-specific adaptation are future work.
- The classical-style expert system does not establish generalization to modern
  generative models or individual semantic meanings.

## Allowed manuscript use
