## Source claims assessed

- StudyU separates digital N-of-1 study design from the participant application
  and backend study record.
- The paper discusses repeated crossover periods, randomization, blinding,
  carryover, participant progress, electronic consent, analysis, and returning
  results.
- Its implementation is precedent for a versioned digital protocol and
  participant-facing execution boundary.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The article presents a platform and authors' vision; it does not validate
  Antidote's audio conditions, measures, analysis, or safety model.
- StudyU's centralized architecture and health-intervention framing do not
  establish Antidote's local-first privacy or clinical status.
- Randomization and digital execution do not remove expectancy, self-researcher,
  time-trend, or carryover threats.

## Allowed manuscript use

Cite as peer-reviewed precedent for digitally separating study definition,
assignment, repeated participant interactions, measurements, and analysis while
retaining explicit methodological concerns.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not cite StudyU as evidence that Antidote's protocol is safe, clinically
valid, causally identified, privacy-preserving, or ready for participant use.
