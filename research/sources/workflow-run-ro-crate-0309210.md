## Source claims assessed

- Workflow Run RO-Crate extends RO-Crate and Schema.org to represent execution
  provenance at several workflow granularities.
- The profiles can bundle workflow definitions, executions, inputs, outputs,
  code, and related metadata as interoperable research objects.
- The paper reports implementations across six workflow systems and describes
  alignment with W3C PROV.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The examples concern computational workflows rather than person-sensitive
  affective audio sessions.
- Packaging metadata does not ensure that a run is reproducible on other
  hardware or that its claims are valid.
- Participant privacy, minimization, redaction, and permission checks remain
  outside the profile's guarantee.

## Allowed manuscript use

Use as peer-reviewed precedent for packaging computational lineage and related
artifacts into a traceable research export.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not claim that RO-Crate establishes privacy review, semantic correctness,
reproducibility by itself, or a complete Antidote export implementation.
