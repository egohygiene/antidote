## Source claims assessed

- JASCO combines a global text description with time-local symbolic and audio
  conditions, including chords, melody, drums, and full-mix representations.
- The authors report objective and human evaluation of generation quality and
  condition adherence.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The reviewed artifact is a preprint and its claims have not been reproduced
  by Antidote.
- Temporal conditioning is not synonymous with live streaming or immediate
  mid-playback prompt changes.
- Licensing, local hardware, duration, continuation, and artifact rights need
  a separate implementation audit.

## Allowed manuscript use

Use as an emerging-system precedent for global semantic intent plus local
time-varying musical controls.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not cite it as proof of streaming generation, seamless interpolation,
production readiness, open-source status, or emotional effect.
