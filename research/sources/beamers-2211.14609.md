## Source claims assessed

- BEAMERS combines short-window commercial EEG features, song features, and a
  user-designated desired emotion change to predict response before music
  recommendation.
- The preprint explicitly models variation in how the same person responds to
  the same song and reports prediction accuracy above 0.85 under its design.
- It is precedent for user-specified direction and person-dependent response,
  not for generated audio.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- This is a preprint and the reported metric has not been independently
  reproduced here.
- It recommends existing songs and does not expose Antidote's proposed semantic
  journey, consent projection, acoustic realization test, or provenance chain.
- EEG-derived features remain fallible observations, not objective emotion.

## Allowed manuscript use

Cite as adjacent preprint evidence that desired emotion variation and
person-dependent song response have already been modeled in EEG-based music
recommendation.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not use its reported accuracy as a general measure of emotion inference or
as evidence of clinical effectiveness. Do not claim that user-selected emotion
direction or person-variable song response is unique to Antidote.
