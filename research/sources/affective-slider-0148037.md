## Source claims assessed

- The Affective Slider is a digital two-slider self-report instrument for
  pleasure and arousal.
- The paper compares the instrument with the Self-Assessment Manikin in an
  online picture-rating task and reports strong correspondence for the two
  dimensions.
- The design targets quick use on modern digital interfaces and supplies
  implementation guidance.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- Validation used picture ratings, not generated music, an unfolding audio
  journey, a moment-specific desired transition, or repeated N-of-1 sessions.
- Pleasure and arousal do not capture personal meaning, mixed emotion,
  usefulness, mismatch, burden, or harm.
- Antidote's additional appraisal items are exploratory and are not validated by
  this source.

## Allowed manuscript use

Use to support the choice of low-burden digital pleasure/valence and arousal
self-report items, while retaining description, intensity, appraisal, and
missingness as separate fields.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not claim that the Affective Slider validates Antidote's complete response
instrument, identifies objective emotion, or supplies a clinical endpoint.
