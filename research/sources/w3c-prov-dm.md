## Source claims assessed

- PROV-DM defines a conceptual data model for provenance centered on entities,
  activities, and agents.
- It defines relationships for generation, use, derivation, attribution,
  association, delegation, and provenance bundles.
- Its concepts support exchanging provenance descriptions across systems.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- A provenance assertion can be incomplete, incorrect, or dishonestly created.
- The standard does not define Antidote's consent, privacy, audio, response, or
  research-evidence semantics.
- Antidote does not currently claim a complete PROV-DM serialization or
  conformance profile.

## Allowed manuscript use

Use as the normative vocabulary anchor for entities, activities, agents, and
derivation links in Antidote's proposed provenance graph.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not claim that a PROV-shaped record proves truth, authorship, consent,
correct interpretation, reproducibility, or Antidote conformance.
