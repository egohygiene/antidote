## Source claims assessed

- The affective music player learns probabilistic, person-specific response
  models from peripheral physiology and each listener's own music library.
- It selects music using current affect and an affective goal while preserving
  idiosyncratic preferences, associations, and memories.
- The evaluation repeatedly sampled three participants over multiple weeks in a
  noisy workplace setting.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The sample is extremely small and all participants were men.
- The system selects pre-existing songs rather than generating an interpretable
  continuous acoustic journey.
- Physiological models are noisy and do not establish a person's true emotion
  or therapeutic need.

## Allowed manuscript use

Cite as peer-reviewed precedent for current-to-goal selection, longitudinal
within-person affect modeling, and personal musical associations.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not claim that within-person affective music learning, current-to-goal
personalization, or personal associations are unique to Antidote. Do not treat
three participants as efficacy evidence.
