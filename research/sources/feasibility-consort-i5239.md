## Source claims assessed

- The extension addresses randomized pilot and feasibility trials conducted in
  preparation for a later definitive randomized trial.
- It distinguishes feasibility objectives and progression decisions from
  premature tests of intervention effectiveness.
- Transparent reporting includes prespecified feasibility outcomes, criteria
  used to judge whether to proceed, and changes made after the pilot.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- Antidote's H1 plan is a single-participant blocked series, not the randomized
  pilot-RCT design governed by this extension.
- Progression criteria are design decisions; meeting them cannot establish
  benefit, safety, or readiness for clinical use.
- The guidance does not validate Antidote's custom burden, mismatch, or harm
  items.

## Allowed manuscript use

Use to justify centering technical integrity, collection completeness, burden,
safety review, and an explicit stop/revise/proceed decision rather than an
effectiveness conclusion.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not describe the planned Antidote series as a definitive randomized trial or
use feasibility criteria as surrogate evidence of efficacy or clinical safety.
