## Source claims assessed

- The system detects a current affective state from EEG and peripheral
  physiology, selects a target trajectory, and generates music toward that
  target using algorithmic composition and case-based reasoning.
- Eight participants completed the longitudinal multi-session evaluation; the
  article reports three-class detection accuracy up to 65% and above-chance
  affect modulation.
- The design includes individual models and therefore precedes broad Antidote
  claims about closed loops, state-to-target journeys, generated interventions,
  or longitudinal personalization.

## Limitations and unresolved questions

- The final sample is small and followed attrition from the initially enrolled
  group; results do not establish population-level or clinical effectiveness.
- Generated output was deliberately constrained and the authors report signal,
  artifact, and classification limitations.
- This record does not establish an exact analogue of Antidote's explicit
  semantic controls, consent-scoped contextual memory, acoustic verification,
  or provenance model.

## Allowed manuscript use

Cite as strong peer-reviewed precedent for a longitudinal affective
brain-computer music loop connecting current state, target state, generated
music, and person-specific updating. Attribute all performance values to the
study.

## Prohibited manuscript use

Do not describe Antidote as the first closed-loop, state-to-target, generative,
or longitudinally personalized affective music system. Do not use this small
study as clinical evidence.
